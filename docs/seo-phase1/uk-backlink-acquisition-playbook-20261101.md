# UK glazing backlinks: evidence-led acquisition playbook

**Checked 1 Nov 2026 — research status, not proof of acquired links.** This playbook supports Veltrix's existing [SEO master tracker](https://github.com/SunnyButt09000/daniweb/issues/29). All outreach to an external organisation requires a genuinely relevant, approved claim and source content. It is not a directive to submit bulk links, request paid dofollow posts or claim qualifications.

## What counts and what does not

- **Inbound earned link:** a third party chooses to link from an appropriate published webpage to Veltrix. A prospect name, contact page, pitch email, paid directory or page on Veltrix **is not** an acquired backlink.
- **Internal contextual links:** useful relevant links between Veltrix's own pages. Google advises crawlable HTML anchors, descriptive wording and at least one inbound site link to every important URL; no magic count. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable
- **Unacceptable shortcut:** paid PageRank links, bulk "1000 backlinks" marketplaces, private blog networks, unrequested guest-post spam, automated forum comments, irrelevant directory spam, hidden/over-optimised footer links, obligatory reciprocal exchanges, fabricated testimonials. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- **Sponsored mention:** if there is a legitimate advertising/sponsorship reason, paid placements must use appropriate `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`. Neither is a promised ranking shortcut.

## Content worth pitching — only after verifying evidence

1. **Glass-only vs full window replacement:** explain actual unit-vs-frame distinction and practical homeowner quote checklist; use `/repairs/glass-and-panel-replacement/`, with real anonymised before/after photos if available.
2. **Misted glazing, seal failures and energy performance:** link the existing `/guides/misted-double-glazing-repair-or-replace/` and service page; get knowledgeable qualified review of technical assertions.
3. **Door repairs vs replacement:** photo-documented example of a real gearbox, hinge or roller fault and a safe explanation of compatible parts, avoiding fictitious savings percentages.
4. **UK-wide window replacement compliance:** original checklist segmented by the four nations, with authoritative links; no blanket claim every installer is FENSA registered.
5. **Case-study pair with an actual installer:** roles, timeframe, location (appropriately coarse), photos and homeowner permission; editorial publication decided by partner, not conditioned on a link.

These are *topics*, not an instruction to generate fake projects or mass submit generic articles.

## 30-day controlled outreach process

**Days 1–3 — Vet actual assets and the business.** Confirm real supplier brands, fitter/partner relationships, permissioned project photos and accurate company identity. Check any existing first-party referring domains (GSC Links report where accessible). Never invent missing input.

**Days 4–10 — Prepare two evidence-backed pitches.** Use an existing useful guide and one independently verifiable piece of original evidence, with photographs only if approved. Ask editors whether the story would serve readers; editors control publication and links. One considered pitch per suitable organisation; no automated bulk mailing.

**Days 11–21 — Carefully approach qualifying partners.** Invite a factual project/stockist profile if it is genuinely useful, not "exchange links" or promises of mutual ranking improvements. Only apply to verified eligible directory/association programs if there is independent commercial value.

**Days 22–30 — Verify and measure.** Record actual source URL, destination URL, site relevance, relationship, editorial/published date, `rel` attributes, live status and referral enquiries. GSC's Links report is delayed/incomplete; cross-check public pages. Stop low-quality tactics rather than scaling them.

## Measurement and prioritisation

Use this simple *internal* 0–12 scoring rubric: topical relevance (0–3), factual relationship/credibility (0–3), genuine visitor/referral opportunity (0–3), practical effort/eligibility (0–3; 3 = ready). **This is not Ahrefs DR or Google ranking score.** Pursue 9–12 scores with permissions and evidence first, avoid bulk links regardless of the score. Track links actually earned, not prospects pitched.

## Operational controls

- No business external emails or public submissions automatically made: company ownership, editorial facts, supplier names and approved project rights must be confirmed before representation.
- No fake Manchester address, unearned certification, misleading installer availability, made-up installation count or customer testimonials.
- No paid services authorised. Store lead/contact data securely if collected; do not add hidden tracking without privacy review.
- Build and test each existing SEO service page as source changes; compare with first-party queries and real booking outcomes.
- Some SEO effect takes time; **no promise of measurable Google traffic in days**.
