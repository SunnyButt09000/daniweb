# Veltrix — UK Windows & Doors Competitive Intelligence / Growth Strategy

**Research date:** 27 October 2026. **Status:** internal research for planning, not public website copy.  
**Companion dataset:** [Competitor registry (82 initial entries)](./competitor-registry-uk-2026.csv). There is no claim that these are all UK companies. Business status, web offers, product prices and geographic coverage change; confidence is indicated per entry.

## 1. Scope and definitions

Include all organisations competing for relevant customer attention or spend:
1. National replacement-window and door installers; **avoid double-counting brands controlled by the same parent** (Which? reports Anglian, Everest and Safestyle within ASHI Group).
2. Regional and local window fitters, door installers, glass replacement and uPVC repair firms.
3. Window and door **supply-only** e-commerce companies with online configurators, custom dimensions, colour options, direct-to-consumer or trade checkout.
4. Specialist glass-unit and composite/bifold door sellers, including product brands with installer networks.
5. Builders' merchants, DIY retailers and trade joinery suppliers. **Indirect competitors for product spend and builder relationships**; not automatically installers.
6. Glazing quote comparison sites and trade directories: competing for organic attention or acting as alternative lead sources, not necessarily fulfilling installation.
7. General builders/developers, roofline/extension contractors and property maintenance firms where evidence shows they actually quote/install windows or doors; also potential B2B clients or partners.

Do **not** assert every listed merchant or platform offers uPVC window fitting. A directory-listed business is not independently inspected at its official site. Sources and validation level appear in the CSV.

## 4. Competitive scoring rubric (do not fabricate scores)

For every registry company investigate the following with dated source URLs. Score **0–5 only when direct evidence exists**, else `Unknown`.

1. Price transparency: displayed *configured* product-only price vs installed price, VAT, delivery.
2. Quote friction: clicks/time from product discovery to viable enquiry; forms, file uploads, WhatsApp send completion.
3. Product breadth: actual uPVC, composite, bifold, sash, aluminium, glazing units, fitting and repair lines.
4. Customer confidence: verified reviews, named trading entity, photographs of completed jobs, compliant credentials.
5. Manufacturer warranty vs fitter workmanship vs IBG: term, guarantor, conditions, effective coverage.
6. Region reliability: claimed locations, evidence of installers, postcode acceptance and date availability.
7. Trade/B2B: product trade accounts, batch quoting, project price books, VAT invoices.
8. SEO/AEO/GEO: useful pages, human answers, schema parity, correct source details, indexability.
9. Mobile experience: configurator, page clarity, speed, call/WhatsApp CTA.
10. After-sales: spare parts, emergency repairs, contact route, written escalation and cancellation procedures.
11. Lead tracking: real submitted messages, reception acknowledgement, CRM and missed-call follow-up.
12. Differentiation: specificity and evidence rather than 'best prices', fabricated customer reviews or unproven accreditation.

Research should end with an **evidence-backed gap**, a small recommended feature, estimated effort and acceptance test. Label assumptions.

## 5. Prioritised improvements — not blanket feature parity

### P0: truthful business proof and operational readiness (before broad advertising or production changes)

- **Manufacturer product guarantee:** Owner confirms 10-year manufacturer's guarantee on **new products**; actual supplier name, warranty PDF, model coverage and claim terms **not supplied**. State that manufacturer is guarantor; do not assert 10 years of workmanship or pretend insurance-backed guarantee exists. See [ASA guarantee guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/guarantees-and-warranties.html).
- **IBG/FENSA dependency:** Owner says Veltrix has *not* arranged IBG. This must not be advertised. [FENSA's installer-type guidance](https://fensahelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360059198434-Installer-types-within-FENSA) says an IBG is mandatory for FENSA standard installers working for private homeowners. A future FENSA partner using that route must provide it as required; verify for each job and never promise nonexistent policy. [FENSA IBG](https://fensahelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/1500000934341-Insurance-Backed-Guarantee-IBG).
- Establish written business relationship with each partner; identity, covered area/postcodes, authorised services, survey standards, insurance/registration/compliance, who contracts and pays, who handles customer data, aftercare and complaints.
- Confirm whether **product supply only** is genuinely offered, supported by factory fulfilment and returns terms, before introducing online product checkout.

### P1: increase conversions on traffic that already arrives

- **Quote journey:** customer selects `supply only` / `supply & fit` / `repair` where actually available. For fitting capture postcode, product style, number, rough sizes, photo, earliest desired date; clear consent for partner contact. For supply only capture delivery postcode and exact specification.
- Quote builder should save/produce an accurate **provisional** schedule and show 'Request sent' only if server/inbox actually received it. Current WhatsApp deep-link opening and manually attached PDFs are not proof of delivery.
- Provide simple, fair **what is included/excluded** sections: product, fitting, survey, waste, certification, travel, delivery, VAT, warranty and aftercare; no unsupported free-survey guarantee for all postcodes.
- Add case studies and photos ONLY after confirmed actual jobs and permission. Do not invent reviews.
- Build a lightweight CRM/lead record after privacy and consent approvals. Measure genuine received enquiries, qualified visits, quotations, conversion and customer-acquisition cost.
- Clarify written manufacturer guarantee by selected product model; no invented manufacturer brand, supplier credentials or document.

### P2: topic-focused UK SEO with genuinely useful content

- Improve existing windows, composite doors, misted glass, locks, installation and quote hubs with proof-first answers and **real** product specifications.
- Build original buyer comparison guides: `supply-only vs fitted windows`, `repair vs replace sealed units`, `where installation prices come from`, `how to compare manufacturer warranty with fitting warranty`.
- No wholesale republishing competitor comparison pages and no AI-generated city doorway pages. [Google doorway/scaled content policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- Publish city pages **only** once real coverage and unique local proof exists.

### P3: builder and installer partner/trade acquisition

- Offer a private **builder / installer / landlord** quote intake with multiple units and product-only/installed option, conditional on commercial readiness.
- Build manufacturer and trade discount relations with written pricing and terms; no unapproved brand partnerships or protected trademarks on site.
- Vet installers against actual location, scheme and handoff; start with demonstrably serviceable areas, expand geographically based on demand and margin.
- Eventually let builder customers save schedules and reorder; prioritise only after repeat orders prove demand.

### P4: measurement and competitor updates

- Monitor only publicly available official pages and legally accessible directories. Never bypass logins, robots restrictions or scrape private/customer data.
- For **top 15 direct competitors**, review **monthly**: changes to product pages, public price examples, delivery terms, new warranties, location claims and conversion flow.
- For other registry entries, check quarterly or when Veltrix opens a newly verified service region.
- Capture screenshot/date or stable webpage link, original fact, confidence, last verification time and next action. Do not copy competitor photography, text, testimonials or customer data.
- Watch GSC and Bing Search Performance for actual Veltrix search queries; add competitors surfacing on those queries. Use live SERP research to identify **organic rivals**, not only marketplace brands.
- Expiry signals: pricing 30 days; offers 14 days; credentials 90 days; contact/delivery/availability before any customer quote.
- Keep registry version-controlled. Each new discovery gets a source URL and category; deduplicate brand and corporate parent.

## 6. Sales prioritisation hypotheses

- **Fastest test:** repairs & misted units where there is an urgent and concrete customer fault; only launch after repair installer coverage verified.
- **Higher value / more complex:** complete house windows and composite doors; user needs genuine installation assurance, not just a low supply-only advertised price.
- **Scalable B2B:** builders/renovators repeatedly buying materials; requires manufacturer pricing, reliable logistics and trusted trade credit/terms.
- **Differentiated proposition to test:** *clear product specification and quote request + transparent vetted fitting process + written manufacturer coverage*. This is a hypothesis; not a factual current guarantee of a UK-wide network.

## 7. Seven-stage customer conversion funnel and KPIs

Discover search → choose product/repair → size or photo input → request with region → coverage & partner verification → accepted written quote/contract → delivery/installation & aftercare.

Measure (actual receipts only): search impressions by cluster; organic clicks; quote starts; genuine requests received; postcode coverage-qualified requests; sent written quotes; paid orders; gross margin; installer completion; complaint and warranty events. **No invented traffic/volume, revenue, costs or conversion improvements.**

## 8. Required source discipline

| Source type | What it proves | What it DOES NOT prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official company website | Company currently advertises service, item, feature or price | That the company actually performs perfectly or that price applies to Veltrix |
| Which? / independent market study | Named competitor/brand ownership or sample result | Live installation slot, current quote, verified product testing beyond study |
| Local search business listing | Candidate local supplier and public reputation summary | Confirmed customer contract or operational capacity |
| Directory listing | Seller advertises in category | That the seller has an official site, verified certificates or nationwide coverage |
| Unverified external traffic estimate | Potential research prompt | Actual visitors, customers, profits, SEO strength |
| Owner-confirmed Veltrix facts | Business-intent specification | Signed partner capacity or documentary manufacturer terms if missing |

**Competition is not a one-time 'all companies done' task.** The 82-row initial register is a reproducible starting point. Expand to one entry per true competitor found by UK-wide/category/local city searches, de-duplicate, then triage direct vs indirect.
