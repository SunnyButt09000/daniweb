## Top five actions to release, in sequence

1. Obtain manufacturer's **name and 10-year written product warranty** and correct any precise coverage/exclusion discrepancies.
2. Identify at least one real fitting partner; verify who handles FENSA/building-control paperwork, insurance and callback responsibilities.
3. Establish reliable lead receipt and privacy-safe handoff; don't assume WhatsApp open means message sent.
4. Collect genuine installation/repair photos, testimonials and product brochures.
5. Compare actual same-spec products and fitted quotations before deciding whether instant pricing is commercially safe.

## Nonnegotiable release checks

- This backlog is internal and should not be rendered as customer-facing marketing.
- Never imply free, available, certified, insured, UK-wide, cheapest, in stock, next-day or guaranteed unless evidence exists for the exact customer job.
- Do not copy competitors' screenshots, code, product photos, reviews or text onto the public website without rights.
- No new paid subscriptions, finance claims, outreach to competitors, private contact harvesting or production deployment without owner approval.
- Track only attributable results: received leads, qualified leads, sales, supply costs, partner completion, aftercare.
